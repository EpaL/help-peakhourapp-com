Before you set up PeakHour on your Mac, make sure you have the following:

  - A Mac running macOS 12.0 Monetary or higher.

  - Your Mac should be connected to the Internet, either via Wi-Fi, Ethernet or USB. Internet access isn't strictly needed for PeakHour to work, but it is required initially to validate the Appstore receipt.

  - An Internet router that supports either UPnP or SNMP for monitoring. These are not mandatory, but you won't be able to monitor your router's Internet speeds if it does not support one of these two features.

To monitor the throughput of routers or devices, they must support either:

  - [SNMP](https://peakhoursupport.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/P5W/pages/9503401/SNMP) (Simple Network Management Protocol) v1, v2c or v3.  
    *and/or*

  - [UPnP](https://peakhoursupport.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/P5W/pages/9503464/UPnP) supporting Internet Gateway Device (IGW) v1.0 or v2.0.

To monitor your Internet latency and performance, your Mac:

  - Must be connected to the Internet with access to both ICMP ping and ICMP traceroute hosts on the Internet.
