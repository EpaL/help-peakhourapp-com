PeakHour works out the grade with fixed rules. The same measurements always give the same grade, whether or not Apple Intelligence is available. The AI only writes the explanation; it never sets the grade.

## What the grade counts

The grade counts five measurements:

- **Latency** — the round trip from this Mac to the internet.
- **Jitter** — how much the latency varies.
- **Packet Loss** — the share of data that doesn't arrive.
- **Max Measured Speed** — the download speed from the last speed test.
- **Responsiveness Under Load** — round trips per minute (RPM) while the link is busy.

Each one lands in a band:

| | Excellent | Good | Fair | Poor |
|-|-----------|------|------|------|
| **Latency** | < 30 ms | 30–60 ms | 60–100 ms | ≥ 100 ms |
| **Jitter** | < 10 ms | 10–30 ms | 30–50 ms | ≥ 50 ms |
| **Packet Loss** | < 0.5% | — | 0.5–2% | ≥ 2% |
| **Speed** | ≥ 500 Mb/s | 50–500 Mb/s | 25–50 Mb/s | < 25 Mb/s |
| **Responsiveness** | ≥ 1,000 RPM | 500–1,000 RPM | 200–500 RPM | < 200 RPM |

No packet loss at all shows as **Perfect**.

Speed and responsiveness come from the [network quality test](/user-guide/settings/dashboard/#network-quality). Until a test has run, the grade leaves responsiveness out and treats speed as Good.

The grade also watches for:

- **A weak Wi-Fi signal** — −75 dBm or weaker.
- **A busy link** — traffic above 90% of the speed that the last test measured, in either direction.
- **Monitors that do not answer** — some of the sites or your ISP's gateway stop answering while others still do.

## From bands to a grade

| Grade | When |
|-------|------|
| **A+** | Every measurement is Excellent, and nothing else holds the grade back. |
| **A** | Every measurement is Good or better, and nothing else holds the grade back. |
| **B+** | Every measurement is Good or better, with one concern such as a weak Wi-Fi signal or a busy link. |
| **B** | A measurement is Fair, or some monitors do not answer. |
| **C** | A measurement is Poor, or there are two or more concerns at once. |
| **D** | Your ISP's gateway does not answer, or a Poor measurement comes with two or more concerns. |
| **F** | No internet site answers. |

The concerns are: a weak Wi-Fi signal, a busy link, monitors that do not answer, packet loss of 0.5% or more, latency over 100 ms, and jitter over 50 ms.

Every grade below an A has at least one reason under **Issues identified** on the [Your Network](/user-guide/advisor/your-network/) page. An A or A+ has none.

## What the grade leaves out

A problem with a single site — for example one slow or unsteady service while the others are fine — does not lower your grade. It is a problem on the route to that site, not on your connection, and you can't fix it from home. The Advisor lists it under **Issues outside your network** on [The Internet](/user-guide/advisor/the-internet/) page instead.
