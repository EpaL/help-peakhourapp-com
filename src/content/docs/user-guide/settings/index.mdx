To open Settings, click the <span class="inline-icon">![Settings button](./images/settings-button.png)</span> button at the bottom of the main window, choose **PeakHour → Settings** from the menu, or press **⌘,** (Command-comma).

![The PeakHour Settings window, with the section list down the left and the Monitors panel selected](./images/settings-window.png)

</div>
