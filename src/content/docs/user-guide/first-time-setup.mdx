## Prerequisites

Before you set up PeakHour on your Mac:

- Check that your system meets the [Requirements](/user-guide/requirements/).
- **Enable UPnP on your Internet router.** PeakHour uses UPnP to discover your router automatically during setup. Most routers ship with UPnP on by default — if yours doesn't, look for an option labelled **UPnP** or **Universal Plug & Play** in your router's admin interface and turn it on before launching PeakHour. See [UPnP Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/upnp/) if your router still isn't discovered.

## Getting Started

When you launch PeakHour for the first time, the First Time Setup Assistant will appear to guide you through the setup process.

<div class="img-md">
![First Time Setup - Introduction](./first-time-setup-images/first-time-setup-intro.png)
</div>

Setup takes a few short steps:

1. **Welcome** — a quick overview of what PeakHour does.
2. **Discover Your Network** — PeakHour finds your router and lets you pick which features to enable.
3. **Choose your sites** — tell PeakHour what you use the Internet for, and choose the sites it measures. This step shows only with the Internet Dashboard on.
4. **Identify Your Internet Provider** — choose whether PeakHour looks up your ISP details (off by default).
5. **Smarter AI Advisor** — choose where the AI Advisor writes its explanations. This step shows only on a Mac that can use Private Cloud Compute.
6. **You're All Set** — confirm your configuration before finishing.
7. **Stay in Touch** — optional: subscribe to updates or follow PeakHour on social.

If you've previously used PeakHour on another Mac with iCloud sync enabled, see [Restoring from iCloud](#restoring-from-icloud) — setup can apply those settings in a single click.

## 1. Welcome

The Welcome screen introduces the three main views you'll work with in PeakHour: bandwidth monitoring for your router, ISP and internet endpoints; the Internet Dashboard; and the menu bar widget. Click **Get Started** to continue.

## 2. Discover Your Network

<div class="img-md">
![Discover Your Network step](./first-time-setup-images/first-time-setup-discover.png)
</div>

PeakHour scans your local network for routers and gateways that support UPnP. If a device is found and is reporting usage correctly, it appears under **Devices Found** with a **Reporting** badge. If your router doesn't appear, see [Can't find your Internet router?](#cant-find-your-internet-router) below.

Below the devices list, choose which features to enable:

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Internet Dashboard & AI Advisor** | An at-a-glance view of your network's health and live diagnostics. |
| **Speed Tests** | Periodic speed tests for your speed and your latency under load. See [Speed Test](/user-guide/settings/dashboard/#speed-test). |
| **Usage Monitoring** | Tracks bandwidth consumption against your data plan. |

All three are enabled by default. Click **Continue** to apply them, or **Skip – Set Up Later** to configure things manually from [Settings](/user-guide/settings/) later.

### Can't find your Internet router?

If the **Devices Found** list is empty or your router isn't appearing:

- Make sure UPnP is enabled on your router — see [Prerequisites](#prerequisites). Look for an option labelled **UPnP** or **Universal Plug & Play** in your router's admin interface.
- Restart both your router and PeakHour after enabling UPnP so PeakHour re-runs discovery.
- Some routers don't fully implement the UPnP spec, or implement it poorly. PeakHour validates what your router reports and marks it as **Reporting** when the data looks valid.
- For deeper diagnostics, see [UPnP Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/upnp/).

**Using SNMP instead?** PeakHour can monitor SNMP-enabled routers and other devices, but SNMP doesn't support auto-discovery — SNMP devices won't appear in First Time Setup. Skip the Discover step and add them after setup from [Settings](/user-guide/settings/).

## 3. Choose your sites

PeakHour measures the Internet with a list of [Internet sites](/user-guide/main-view/internet-sites/). This step helps you choose sites that you use. It has two pages.

<div class="img-md">
![The What Do You Use the Internet For? page, with seven cards such as Work & Video Calls, Gaming and Streaming](./first-time-setup-images/first-time-setup-sites-audiences.png)
</div>

**What Do You Use the Internet For?** — click each card that fits: **Work & Video Calls**, **Gaming**, **Streaming**, **AI Tools**, **Cloud & Files**, **Social** or **General**. You can choose more than one, or none. Click **Continue**.

<div class="img-md">
![The Choose Your Sites page, with the suggested sites selected at the top, and more sites in groups under them](./first-time-setup-images/first-time-setup-sites-choose.png)
</div>

**Choose Your Sites** — PeakHour suggests five sites from your choices, with **Cloudflare** first. With no card chosen, it suggests **Cloudflare** only. The suggested sites show selected under **Your Sites**. Click a site to add it or remove it, up to 10 sites. To add a site that isn't in the list, type its host name or address in **Host name or address**, and click **Add Host**. Click **Continue**.

*PeakHour pings each site directly. Your choice stays on this Mac.*

Click **Skip** to keep the default sites: Google, Cloudflare and Netflix. You can change the sites at any time in [Settings → Dashboard → Internet Sites](/user-guide/settings/dashboard/#internet-sites).

## 4. Identify Your Internet Provider

<div class="img-md">
![Identify Your Internet Provider step](./first-time-setup-images/first-time-setup-privacyconsent.png)
</div>

PeakHour can enrich your [Internet Dashboard](/user-guide/main-view/internet-dashboard/) with your provider's name, approximate location, and ISP and router brand logos. It also lets the [PeakHour Advisor](/user-guide/advisor/) show the route to each site. Enabling this lookup is optional.

**Look up ISP details is off by default.** Leave the toggle off to keep PeakHour from contacting any third-party services, or turn it on to identify your provider. You can change this later under [Settings → Dashboard](/user-guide/settings/dashboard/#isp-information).

When enabled, PeakHour uses these services to look up your provider and the route to each site:

| Service | What it provides |
|---------|------------------|
| **ipify** | Detects your public IP address. |
| **MaxMind**, through PeakHour's own lookup service | The network name and approximate location of your IP address, the sites you monitor, and the routers on the way. |
| **RIPE NCC** | The network that a router belongs to, when MaxMind can't name it. |
| **PeeringDB** | A network's web domain, to find its logo. |
| **Brandfetch** | ISP, router and network logos. |

:::note
These services receive internet addresses, network numbers and web domains — not your name, your Mac's name or your Wi-Fi name. For exactly what each one receives, see [ISP Information](/user-guide/settings/isp-information/#what-goes-where).
:::

Click **Learn More** to open the [ISP Information](/user-guide/settings/isp-information/) page, or **Privacy Policy** to read exactly how this information is used, then click **Continue**.

## 5. Smarter AI Advisor

On a Mac with macOS 27 and Apple Intelligence, setup asks where the AI Advisor writes its explanations. Turn on **Private Cloud Compute** to let a larger Apple model write them, or leave it off to keep the AI Advisor on your Mac. You can change it later in [Settings → Dashboard → AI Advisor](/user-guide/settings/dashboard/#ai-advisor). See [Private Cloud Compute](/user-guide/advisor/ask/#private-cloud-compute).

## 6. You're All Set

<div class="img-md">
![You're All Set summary screen](./first-time-setup-images/first-time-setup-youre-all-set.png)
</div>

Review the configuration PeakHour is about to apply:

- **Internet Gateway** — the router PeakHour found in the previous step.
- **Features** — the features you enabled: **Internet Dashboard**, **Speed Test**, **ISP Information** and **Usage Monitoring**.
- **Launch at Login** — toggle on if you'd like PeakHour to start automatically when you log in to your Mac.

Click **Continue** to apply your configuration.

## 7. Stay in Touch

<div class="img-md">
![Stay in Touch step](./first-time-setup-images/first-time-setup-stay-in-touch.png)
</div>

The final step is optional. Join the mailing list for occasional updates and tips (no spam), or follow PeakHour on [X](https://x.com/peakhourapp), [Mastodon](https://mastodon.social/@peakhour) or [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/peakhour.app).

Click **Get Started** to finish setup and start monitoring. 🎉

## After Setup

<div class="img-sm">
![You're All Set popover anchored from the menu bar](./first-time-setup-images/first-time-setup-popover.png)
</div>

Once setup completes, PeakHour starts monitoring in the background and a short popover appears from its menu bar icon confirming everything is up and running. Click anywhere to dismiss it.

### Can't see PeakHour in your menu bar?

PeakHour needs some horizontal space in the menu bar to display its monitors — especially on Macs with the notch (MacBook Pro 14"/16"), where usable menu-bar width is limited. If PeakHour's icons aren't visible, macOS has likely hidden them behind the notch or another app's menu-bar item.

To make room:

- Open **PeakHour Settings → [Menu Bar](/user-guide/settings/menu-bar/)** and disable any Menu Bar Items you don't need by dragging them from **Active Items** back to **Available Items**.
- Or remove other apps' menu-bar icons from **System Settings → Control Center**, or use a menu-bar manager like Bartender or Ice to hide them.

If you can't see PeakHour at all, click its icon in the Dock to open the main window — monitoring continues regardless of whether the menu bar item is visible.

## Restoring from iCloud

<div class="img-md">
![iCloud restore option on the Welcome screen](./first-time-setup-images/first-time-setup-icloud-restore.png)
</div>

If you've previously set up PeakHour on another Mac signed in to the same Apple Account, the Welcome screen detects your saved settings and offers to restore them. Click **Restore & Finish** to apply your existing configuration in a single step, or **Set Up Manually** to go through the regular steps.

## Next Steps

  - To read more about PeakHour's many options, visit [Settings](/user-guide/settings/).
