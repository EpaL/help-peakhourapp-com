The **Advisor** section of the [main window](/user-guide/main-view/) is a short summary of the [PeakHour Advisor](/user-guide/advisor/). It shows how your connection is, without a second window. It sits under the [Internet Dashboard](/user-guide/main-view/internet-dashboard/) by default.

<div class="img-md">

![The Advisor section with the grade, a summary, two reasons, the last speed test with Run Now, and the links to the Advisor pages](./advisor-section-images/advisor-section.png)

</div>

From top to bottom, the section shows:

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Grade** and **summary** | Your [grade](/user-guide/advisor/grade/), from **A+** down to **F**, and a summary of your connection in one or two lines. It is the same grade as the dashboard and the Advisor window. |
| **Reasons** | Up to three issues that hold the grade back, the worst first. A yellow dot marks a **Fair** issue, and a red dot a worse one. Each issue says where the problem is, for example on your Wi-Fi. A grade of **A** or **A+** has no reasons. |
| **Speed test** | Your last speed test, for example **↓ 512 Mb/s ↑ 41 Mb/s · Good**, and how long ago it ran. Before your first test, it reads **No speed test yet**. |
| **Run Now** | Runs a speed test. It is grey while a test can't start. Hover over it to see the reason, for example **Offline**. |
| **Links** | **Your Network**, **The Internet**, **Speed Test** and **History** open the [Advisor window](/user-guide/advisor/) at that page. |

Until PeakHour has its first measurements, the section reads **Gathering data**.

When you collapse the section, its header still shows the grade.

## With the AI Advisor off

With [AI Advisor](/user-guide/settings/dashboard/#ai-advisor) off, the section has no grade, summary or reasons. It still shows your last speed test, **Run Now** and the links to the Advisor window.

## Requirements

The Advisor section needs macOS 26 or later, and the [Internet Dashboard](/user-guide/settings/dashboard/) turned on. On macOS 14 and 15, the section doesn't show, and it isn't in the list of sections.

To hide the section or move it, right-click its header, or use [Settings → Display → Main Window](/user-guide/settings/display/#main-window). See [Customize the main window](/user-guide/main-view/#customize-the-main-window).
