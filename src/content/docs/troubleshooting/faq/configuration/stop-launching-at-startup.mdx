title: "How do I stop PeakHour from launching at startup?"

---

You can control whether PeakHour launches at startup via **Settings → [General](/user-guide/settings/general/) → Start PeakHour at Login**.

![The General settings page showing the Start PeakHour at Login option](./stop-launching-at-startup-images/settings-general.png)

You can also manage this from macOS itself: open **System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions** and remove PeakHour from the **Open at Login** list.
