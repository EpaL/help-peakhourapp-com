---
title: "Can I upgrade from a subscription to a Lifetime license?"
description: "Yes — cancel your subscription, wait for it to expire, then purchase a Lifetime license."
sidebar:
  label: Subscription → Lifetime license
status: reviewed
---

Yes, you can upgrade your license from a yearly subscription to a Lifetime license.

To do this, you must first cancel your existing subscription. To cancel your existing PeakHour subscription, follow the instructions on [Apple's support page](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428).

![The App Store subscription screen showing a cancelled PeakHour subscription and its end date](./upgrade-subscription-to-lifetime-images/cancel-subscription.png)

Once your subscription has been cancelled, wait until your subscription term has expired. After it has expired, the next time you launch PeakHour you will be prompted to choose a purchase option. Under **Lifetime License**, click **Purchase Lifetime**.

![The Unlock PeakHour 6 purchase screen with the Lifetime License option highlighted](./upgrade-subscription-to-lifetime-images/iap-paywall-lifetimechoice.png)
