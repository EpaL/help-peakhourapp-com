You can get PeakHour from the [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/peakhour-network-monitor/id1560576252?mt=12) or from [Setapp](https://setapp.com). The two copies are separate apps. They have the same features, including the [PeakHour Advisor](/user-guide/advisor/).

## What is different

Your Setapp membership gives you the license for the Setapp copy. You buy nothing in PeakHour.

| | Mac App Store copy | Setapp copy |
|---|---|---|
| **License** | A free trial, an Annual Pass or a Lifetime License, bought in PeakHour | Your Setapp membership |
| **Purchases in PeakHour** | Yes | No |
| **Restore Purchases** | Yes | No |
| **Settings → About** | Your license, for example **Lifetime License** | **Setapp** and the status of your membership |
| **Updates** | From the Mac App Store | From Setapp |
| **Features** | All | All |

The Setapp copy has no purchase screen. The [First Time Setup](/user-guide/first-time-setup/) has no **Start Monitoring** step with plans, and no **Stay in Touch** step. PeakHour also shows no **Get Lifetime License…** item, and it doesn't ask you to rate it on the App Store.

## Check your status

Open **Settings → About**. The license card shows **Setapp** and one of these:

- **Active**: your Setapp membership is active.
- **Inactive**: your Setapp membership isn't active. Open Setapp to check your membership.

Setapp manages your membership, so the card has no button to buy, restore or manage a license.

## Sign in to Setapp

PeakHour asks Setapp for the status of your membership. If you're signed out of Setapp, or Setapp isn't installed, Setapp shows its own dialog and asks you to sign in. Sign in with your Setapp account.

## Updates

Setapp delivers the updates for the Setapp copy. The Mac App Store updates only the Mac App Store copy.

## Don't run both copies

:::caution
Don't run the Mac App Store copy and the Setapp copy at the same time. They share your iCloud settings and the [PeakHour Remote](/user-guide/settings/remote/) port. When both copies are open, PeakHour shows an alert. Quit one of them.
:::

## Move from the Mac App Store copy

To move your monitors and settings to the Setapp copy, see [Move from the App Store copy to Setapp](/troubleshooting/faq/purchasing-licensing/move-from-app-store-to-setapp/). That page also tells you what to do with your App Store license.
