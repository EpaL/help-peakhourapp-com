When your grade is below an A, the [PeakHour Advisor](/user-guide/advisor/your-network/) lists each issue that holds it back, with a step to try. For the issues below, that step links to a page here with more detail.

- **[Improve responsiveness under load](/troubleshooting/fix-a-grade/responsiveness-under-load/)** — your connection slows down while something uploads or downloads. On Wi-Fi, a test over a cable comes first, then Smart Queue Management (SQM).
- **[Improve a weak Wi-Fi signal](/troubleshooting/fix-a-grade/weak-wifi-signal/)** — your Mac gets a weak signal, or the Wi-Fi link loses data.
- **[When your connection is almost full](/troubleshooting/fix-a-grade/connection-near-capacity/)** — something on your network uses most of your download or upload speed.
