Explanations of the common terms and concepts behind PeakHour's monitoring:

- **[SNMP](/troubleshooting/reference/snmp/)** — the Simple Network Management Protocol, PeakHour's most capable monitoring protocol.
- **[SNMP Community](/troubleshooting/reference/snmp-community/)** — the password/shared key used to query SNMP devices.
- **[High Capacity Counters](/troubleshooting/reference/high-capacity-counters/)** — 64-bit counters that keep usage accurate on fast links.
- **[UPnP](/troubleshooting/reference/upnp/)** — Universal Plug and Play, the protocol most home routers speak.
- **[Usage Monitoring](/troubleshooting/reference/usage-monitoring/)** — how PeakHour measures usage, and what affects its accuracy.
