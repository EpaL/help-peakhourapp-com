Troubleshooting problems when discovering or monitoring your router over [UPnP](/troubleshooting/reference/upnp/):

- **[My router supports UPnP but PeakHour can't see it, or the data doesn't seem right](/troubleshooting/upnp/router-not-detected-or-data-wrong/)** — many routers implement UPnP telemetry poorly.
- **[PeakHour says UPnP can't be initialised, but I've already closed all running apps](/troubleshooting/upnp/upnp-cant-be-initialised/)** — another app is holding the UPnP broadcast port.
- **[Testing your router's UPnP with MiniUPnP](/troubleshooting/upnp/test-router-with-miniupnp/)** — verify what your router actually reports, independently of PeakHour.

New to UPnP? The [UPnP](/troubleshooting/reference/upnp/) reference page explains the basics.
