This will completely remove your PeakHour settings, including your history data.

If you wish to retain your settings, we suggest making a backup of the files before you delete them. Alternatively, enable [iCloud Sync](https://peakhoursupport.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/P5D/pages/37388289/iCloud) before running the commands below, then restore the configuration later once you have confirmed it is safe to do so.

To reset your configuration, follow these steps:

1.  Make sure PeakHour is stopped / not running.

2.  Open Terminal (the easiest way is to click the Spotlight magnifying glass in the top-right corner of the screen, type 'Terminal' and hit Enter).

3.  Copy and paste the following and hit Enter.
    
    ``` syntaxhighlighter-pre
    rm -r ~/Library/Containers/com.digitician.peakhour5
    ```

4.  Reboot your Mac.
