When first launching PeakHour, you see the following error message:

![2517DCA6-F7FA-4A70-B402-453D1415A69A-20240111-205400.png](./error-initializing-appstore-offerings-images/2517dca6-f7fa-4a70-b402-453d1415a69a-20240111-205400.png)

The most common cause for this issue is problems with the Mac Appstore on your Mac.

It is usually a simple fix. Here are some steps to try:

1.  Reboot your Mac

2.  Try disabling any DNS blockers (e.g. pihole) or filtering firewalls/proxies on your network.

3.  Log out and back in to the Mac Appstore. To do this:
    
    1.  Launch the Mac Appstore
    
    2.  Choose **Store** \> **Sign Out**
    
    3.  Click **Sign In** at the bottom left corner of the window and follow prompts.

If your issue still persists after performing the steps above, reach out to us at <support@peakhourapp.com> and we’ll be happy to assist.
