To find out if you device is compatible, the easiest way is to download PeakHour from the Appstore and then head on over to our [Getting Started](https://peakhoursupport.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/documentation/pages/655425/Getting+Started) page.

Key points regarding compatibility

  - PeakHour can monitor throughput of a wide variety of capable devices: routers, wireless access points, servers, printers, PCs, Macs etc.

  - The key to being able to monitor them is that they support either the SNMP or UPnP protocol.
    
      - Most Internet routers support UPnP.
    
      - Some Internet routers support SNMP as well.

  - Mac, Windows and Linux PCs support SNMP allowing them to be monitored directly.
    
      - See [I'd like to monitor my Mac/PC/Linux devices. How can I enable them?](/troubleshooting/frequently-asked-questions-faq/monitoring-compatibility/i-d-like-to-monitor-my-mac-pc-linux-devices-how-can-i-enable-them/) on how to enable SNMP on various devices.

  - For servers, NAS devices and printers, see the documentation for those devices.

### Expert Tip

If your router isn't compatible with PeakHour and you're not afraid of a little tinkering, open source replacement firmwares such as [DD-WRT](http://www.dd-wrt.com/site/index) or [Tomato](http://www.myopenrouter.com/category/31/Tomato) may provide an alternative option. We've tested the latest DD-WRT ourselves in-house and find it offers excellent SNMP support which works well with PeakHour.
