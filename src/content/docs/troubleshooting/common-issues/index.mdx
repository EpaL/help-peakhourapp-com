The most common problems and how to fix them:

- **[My graph isn't smooth / traffic appears in bursts](/troubleshooting/common-issues/graph-isnt-smooth/)** — jagged, on/off graphs caused by slow-updating routers.
- **[Adjust Scaling Factor](/troubleshooting/common-issues/adjust-scaling-factor/)** — when a device reports double or half the actual traffic.
- **[How do I check if PeakHour is reporting data accurately?](/troubleshooting/common-issues/check-reporting-accuracy/)** — sanity-check PeakHour's numbers with a Network Quality test.

## Working with support

- **[Gathering Diagnostic Logs and Crash Dumps](/troubleshooting/common-issues/gathering-diagnostic-logs/)** — capture logs and crash reports for analysis.
- **[How do I archive and send a copy of my PeakHour configuration?](/troubleshooting/common-issues/archive-and-send-configuration/)** — package your configuration for support.

For protocol-specific problems, see [SNMP Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/snmp/) and [UPnP Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/upnp/).
