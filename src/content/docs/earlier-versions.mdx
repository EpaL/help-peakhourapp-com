This help centre documents **PeakHour 6**. The separate documentation sites for
PeakHour 2, 3, 4 and 5 have been retired, and their pages now redirect here.

If you followed a link or a bookmark to one of those pages, this is why it did
not take you where you expected.

## Still running an earlier version?

PeakHour 2, 3 and 4 are no longer supported and receive no further updates. Most of
what they documented has an equivalent in PeakHour 6, though names and locations
have changed — the [User Guide](/user-guide/) is the place to start.

- **Upgrading from PeakHour 4?** There is a discounted upgrade path. See
  [How do I upgrade from PeakHour 4?](/troubleshooting/faq/purchasing-licensing/upgrade-from-peakhour-4/)
- **Need an older build?** Download links for the last releases of PeakHour 2, 3
  and 4 are on
  [Where can I download older versions of PeakHour?](/troubleshooting/faq/purchasing-licensing/download-older-versions/)
